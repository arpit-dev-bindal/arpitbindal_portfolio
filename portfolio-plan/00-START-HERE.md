## Recommended direction

**From interface to infrastructure.**

This concept connects Arpit's work across product design, full-stack engineering, Web3, and applied AI.
The site shows the person, the work, and the decisions behind each product.
A visitor can read the main facts without a 3D interaction.

The first screen pairs a clear statement with a custom metal structure.
Four separate layers represent interface, systems, integration, and intelligence.
Scroll motion brings these layers together.
Real project images then replace the abstract introduction.
Light sections give detailed work and career facts more space.
The final section returns to the dark surface with a direct contact link.

**Proposed opening text:**

> Arpit Bindal
>
> I build complete digital products.
>
> My work connects interface design, full-stack systems, Web3, and applied AI.
>
> View my work

The navigation also provides a direct Contact link.
We will confirm any availability statement before publication.
